MY EXPERTISE

Why founders work with me

• 01

Context reading to product scale

Most environments are imperfect, political or simply not ready for the solutions people try to impose on them. Scaling a product starts with reading that reality. I look at how people work, where information breaks and what the team can actually absorb. From there, I design patterns, rituals and decision structures that scale, the kind that turn individual effort into something the whole organization can carry.

✔ Reads team reality, not theory
✔ Spots where decisions break
✔  Matches solutions to actual maturity

• 02

AI that makes sense

I treat AI as a long conversation between technology, ethics and real‑world consequences. Before touching models, I look at purpose, trust and the cost of complexity. I avoid trends that create noise and focus on building AI systems that solve something concrete without becoming heavier than the problem they were meant to fix.

✔ Complexity only when needed
✔ ROI‑driven use cases
✔ Tech + ethics aligned

• 03

SaaS, B2B and complex industries

I work in environments shaped by regulation, legacy systems and decision cycles that move slowly for a reason. These places demand clarity, not speed for its own sake. I design product strategies that respect the constraints, reduce friction and keep growth from turning into a service shop disguised as software.

✔ Works with regulation, not against it
✔ Friction reduced across teams
✔  Growth kept product‑led, not service‑led
